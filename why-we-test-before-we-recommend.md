# Why We Test Before We Recommend

Hyperping Solutions believes recommendations should be based on observation rather than assumptions.

Many connectivity problems appear similar on the surface but have different root causes.

Before suggesting a solution, we prefer to:

* Observe the environment
* Verify the reported symptoms
* Compare different routes and conditions
* Eliminate obvious causes first
* Test changes whenever possible

This approach often takes slightly longer, but it reduces unnecessary changes and improves confidence in the final result.

We believe testing should come before recommendations.

Simple observations are often more valuable than complex theories.
