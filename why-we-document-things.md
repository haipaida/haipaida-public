# Why We Document Things

Documentation helps us understand what was done, why it was done, and what was learned.

We believe documentation is an important part of long-term maintenance.

Good documentation can:

- Reduce repeated mistakes
- Simplify troubleshooting
- Preserve knowledge over time
- Improve consistency

Technology changes, but clear documentation remains valuable.

For us, documentation is not an afterthought. It is part of the operational process.
