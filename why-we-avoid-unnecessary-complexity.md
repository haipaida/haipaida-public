# Why We Avoid Unnecessary Complexity

Complexity often appears impressive.

It can also create additional points of failure.

When designing or maintaining systems, we prefer solutions that are easy to understand, operate, and troubleshoot.

A simpler solution is not always possible.

When complexity is required, it should exist for a clear reason.

If two approaches achieve similar results, we generally prefer the one that is easier to maintain over time.

We believe simplicity is not the absence of capability.

It is the removal of unnecessary parts.
